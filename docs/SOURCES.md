# Data and sources

- Geometry: 182 timber members of the Hardleaf Pavilion, Gramazio Kohler Research, ETH Zurich (MIT licence, see `data/HARDLEAF-LICENSE.txt`). https://github.com/gramaziokohler/coding_architecture_fs26_focus_work
- Light: shaded share of 25 cm ground cells and direct radiation (Meinel and Meinel 1976, simplified clear sky; no diffuse light, cloud or reflection).
- Date, hours, site, seating zone, number of people and the 50% shade target are assumptions and placeholders.
- Geometry only: structure, joints and re-notching are not checked. Nothing here shows that an option carries load.
- Viewer built with three.js (MIT licence, see `vendor/three/LICENSE`).
